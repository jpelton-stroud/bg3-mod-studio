**URL**: `https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/m/enemy-legends-patch`

## Overview
A patch for the Enemy Tacticians mod to enable Legendary compatibility with Honor mode. Due to the vanilla implementation of Honor Mode, this patch is required to enable the Legendary Actions of most enemies who would otherwise have them.
